The publication

About CyberVendorGuide.

Research notes about cybersecurity tools, software categories, and the decisions that surround them.

Security teams face an expanding set of products, overlapping categories, and technical claims that are hard to compare. CyberVendorGuide is a place to make those choices easier to understand.

What we cover

We publish practical guidance on vendor evaluation, category definitions, product selection, and the operational questions that matter after a purchase.

How we write

Our aim is to distinguish claims from evidence, explain trade-offs, and give buyers questions they can use during evaluations. An effective tool is one that meets a real need in a particular environment, not necessarily the one with the longest specification sheet.
